# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and empathetic toward others.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair and appropriate action in response to behavior considered inappropriate. This includes removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and explaining the reasons.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer privately through GitHub: [@romainhr](https://github.com/romainhr). Do not post reports in public issues. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the reporter are respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer will follow these steps, depending on the severity of the behavior:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning explaining the problem and the expected behavior.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behavior, including no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban from any interaction with the project, for a pattern of violations or serious harm.
